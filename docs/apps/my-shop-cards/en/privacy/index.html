Card Archive Privacy Policy

Card Archive ("the App") respects your privacy.

1. Information stored

The card information you register (name, notes, date, address, location, rating and so on) and images are stored on your device. They are not sent to or stored on the developer's servers.

2. Use of device features

The camera is used to photograph cards. Only the photos you choose are brought into the app. Location is used only while you are using the app, to record where a shop is and to sort by "nearest" (location is never collected all the time). Face ID is used to unlock the app; no face or fingerprint data is passed to the app. You can change each permission at any time in iOS Settings.

3. Background removal and text reading

Background removal and reading text from photos are done on your device. Images are not sent to any external service.

4. Maps and address search

When you search for a place from an address or show a map, Apple's map service is used, and the address or location you enter may be sent to Apple. It is handled under Apple's privacy policy.

5. Third parties

We do not provide the information we obtain to third parties.

6. Plus (purchase)

Plus purchases are processed through the Apple App Store. The developer does not receive payment details such as credit card numbers, and no external billing service is used.

7. iCloud sync (Plus)

When you turn on Plus iCloud sync, your cards, images and settings are saved in your own iCloud (a private database) and synced with iPhones that use the same Apple ID. The developer cannot access that content. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.

8. Backup files

An exported backup file is saved only where you choose (Files, iCloud Drive and so on). The developer never receives it.

9. Deleting data

Deleting the app also deletes the card information and images on the device. "Delete all data" in Settings also deletes the data on the device. Data in iCloud can be deleted from iCloud (Manage Storage) in iOS Settings.
