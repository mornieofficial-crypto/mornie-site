Draw & Create: Creature Home Support

About the app

“Draw & Create: Creature Home” (Japanese title: かいてつくる！いきもののすみか) is a game where you build homes for living creatures by tracing the screen with your finger.

Draw grassland or waterside areas in any shape you like, and creatures will come to the places you drew. Flowers, rocks, and other things appear too. The creatures move around, and a sound plays when they appear.

There is no set way to build and no goal. Draw, change, and draw again — enjoy making a habitat that is all your own.

How to play

Use the round buttons on the right side of the screen to choose what to draw.

  • Green button: draws grassland (land)
  • Light blue button: draws waterside areas
  • Soil-colored button: turns the areas you drew back into plain soil
  • Red button (↺): erases everything you drew so you can start over. When “ぜんぶ けしますか？” (“Erase everything?”) appears, choose “はい” (Yes) or “いいえ” (No).

After choosing a button, trace the screen with your finger.

  • When you draw grassland, flowers bloom after a little while and land creatures arrive.
  • When you draw waterside areas, either river and pond creatures or sea creatures arrive. Which ones come changes each time you build.
  • The bigger you draw, the more creatures arrive.
  • Creatures may not come to areas that are too small.

There is no right answer. Build all kinds of habitats — a big pond, a thin river, water in the middle of grassland, and more.

About your drawings

This app does not save the habitats you draw. When you close the app, your drawings are erased.

Troubleshooting

  • If the app does not work properly: close the app, then start it again.
  • If you cannot hear any sound: check your device’s volume and silent mode settings.

If the problem continues, please reach us through the contact information on this support site.
