Draw & Create: Creature Home Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy sets out how “Draw & Create: Creature Home” (Japanese title: かいてつくる！いきもののすみか; the “App”) handles information.

1. Collection of personal information

The App has no user registration or login features. It has no screens for entering personal information such as your name, email address, or age, and it does not collect personal information.

The App is intended for use by children, but it does not collect any information about children either.

2. Storage of game content

Game content, such as the habitats you draw and the creatures that appear, is handled on your device only while you are playing. It is not saved on your device or on any server.

When you close the App, the game content is erased. If you delete the App, no game content from the App remains.

3. Sending information externally

The App does not communicate over the internet. It does not send game content or device information to external servers.

4. Advertising and in-app purchases

The App does not display ads. It has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

5. Analytics and crash reports

The App does not use analytics tools or tools that collect crash reports.

6. External services

The App does not use any external services that collect information. It uses standard features of Expo (an app development framework) to play sound effects, provide vibration, and draw the screen. These features work only on your device and do not send information externally.

7. Device features and permissions

The App does not use the following features:

  • Location
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Photo library
  • Contacts
  • Notifications

The App plays sound effects and uses vibration (on supported devices only), but these do not require your permission and do not collect any information.

8. Information you provide when contacting us

Any information you provide when contacting us is used only to respond to your inquiry and to investigate problems.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may change this Privacy Policy as necessary. If we make changes, we will announce them on this page.
